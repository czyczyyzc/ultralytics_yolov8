Relative to the retrained 14-video add-on baseline, expanded training improves
native-gray recall by 21.20 percentage points, reduces FP by 40.27%, and improves
native mAP50 by 22.31 points. On Video00004, mAP50 improves by 8.05 points and
FP falls from 543 to 419, with zero missed GT instances at confidence 0.03.

## Limitations And Regressions

- Standalone P3 does not improve on every dataset: Video00004 mAP50 decreases
  from 74.52% to 69.35%, with FP increasing from 138 to 249 at confidence 0.03.
  The positive final result concerns the combined frozen-P3/add-on-P2 detector.
- Large-target capability is still inadequate. On the separate 64-image synthetic
  zoom diagnostic set, expanded add-on recall is 6.25% at confidence 0.03 versus
  9.375% for baseline-14. Recall is zero in the 25-50%, 50-80%, and >=80% image-area
  bins. This diagnostic is not a real large-target test set or a selection source.
- The newly generated 856 replacement images were NOT used in this training.
  Their impact on detection metrics has not been measured. They remain positive-only
  review candidates; image integrity does not establish visual realism or efficacy.
- No deployed model was replaced, and no new board FPS claim is made.
