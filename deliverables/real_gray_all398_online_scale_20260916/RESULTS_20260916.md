## Failure, Fix and Recovery

Original failure: 2026-09-16 11:38:36 +0800, P3 epoch 15, batch 479/1428.
The sampler underestimated the integer pixel span of fractional-coordinate
bounding boxes. A tight area bin could then reject all 48 crop proposals and
raise a DataLoader error. This was an augmentation implementation error.

Commit `84a793c` computes the enclosing integer span and provides a legal,
full-target fallback for sampling exhaustion. It does not discard a training
sample or remove its box. Invalid source geometry still raises an error.
Validation: 16 server regression tests passed, plus 199,000 geometry draws
(398 donors x 500 seeds) with no failures; the explicit fallback has a unit test.

The failed status, last checkpoint and results CSV are preserved under
`failure_backup_20260916/` in the run directory. Original `pipeline.log` and
`protocol.json` remain unchanged. Resume PID: 1292464, GPU 6; the existing
approximately 0.6GB Triton service is retained. New log:
`pipeline_resume_20260916.log`. See live `status.json` rather than treating this
dated report as a live progress feed.

Resume restores saved optimizer/EMA/scheduler from completed epoch 14 and reruns
the interrupted epoch 15, then schedules the pending 15 add-on epochs. The
post-restart RNG stream is not bit-identical to uninterrupted training.

## Validation-Shape Caveat

The training-time validator uses automatic rectangular batching rather than a
fixed 960x544 canvas. The existing rectangular resize metadata also makes its
size-bin counts unsuitable for reporting deployment-scale recall. Therefore the
tables above were recomputed offline at an asserted fixed 960x544 shape, rather
than copied from training CSVs. Training selection is left unchanged during
resume to avoid mixing incompatible best-fitness histories. A future protocol
should correct training-time validation shape/metadata and re-evaluate all
references consistently before comparing selected checkpoints.
